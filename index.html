Questions

Will it work with my car?

If your car has a factory screen, there’s a good chance we can add CarPlay. Most of our installs are BMWs, and we also work on Mercedes-Benz, Audi, Land Rover, Porsche, MINI and Jaguar. Use the fit checker or text us a photo of your screen, and we’ll tell you exactly what fits.

Do I keep my factory features?

Yes. Your original controls, factory camera and vehicle menus all keep working. CarPlay and Android Auto are added on top, and you can switch back to the factory system any time.

How much does it cost?

$400 to add CarPlay to your factory screen, or $500 for a new touchscreen, installed. Some cars need a larger screen or extra parts, and we’ll tell you up front. You get your exact price by text before anything is booked.

Where do you install?

We come to you, at home or at work, anywhere in DC, Maryland and Northern Virginia.

What warranty do I get?

Every install comes with a 1-year hardware warranty and lifetime tech support for software or connection issues. If something stops working, text us.

Dealer, Turo host or more than one car?

We plan installs across several vehicles and keep a full vehicle list for dealers.
